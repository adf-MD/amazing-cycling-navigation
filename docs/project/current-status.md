## Manual acceptance status (21 August 2026)

This is the authoritative manual-test ledger. It records verification evidence only; the behaviour contracts remain defined by the sections above and are not restated here.

**Orientation scope (decided 9 September 2026).** Phone **portrait is ACN's only supported and acceptance-tested orientation** — the orientation a bike computer is used in. Every short-landscape acceptance requirement and open checklist entry that previously appeared below has been **retired** as a consequence, and the individual entries have been edited accordingly rather than left standing as open work. This is a product decision, not a technical lock: landscape is not deliberately broken, no rotation-blocking overlay exists, and nothing here claims orientation is locked. Whether to request portrait through the web-app manifest is a separate implementation decision requiring its own installed-iPhone verification, and is not approved by this decision. Landscape evidence already recorded in `history/` is left untouched as the historical account of what was tested at the time.
